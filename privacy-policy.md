# Privacy Policy for Tab Discarder

Tab Discarder operates locally in Chrome and does not collect or transmit browsing data to an external server. The extension requires no account and contains no analytics or advertising service.

The extension uses Chrome local storage to retain your theme preference, protection settings, and protected-domain list. It uses Chrome session storage for the tab IDs, window IDs, and timestamps that support the **Recently awakened** feature. Those session records are reconciled with live tabs and are not retained as browsing history across browser sessions.

The extension reads the tab information required to display tab state and request a manual discard, including titles, addresses, group membership, and state. That information stays within Chrome and is not shared with third parties.
